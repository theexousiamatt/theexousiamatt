Eatly

An evolving digital experience for Eatly, spanning ordering, delivery, vendor journeys and the brand moments around them.

The challenge

Food delivery involves different journeys for customers, vendors and riders. Each experience needs to feel coherent without asking every user to navigate the same interface.

The approach

The work brings the product and website together through recognizable patterns, clear navigation, purposeful imagery and consistent campaign design.
